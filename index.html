Visitor Information

Currency

U.S. dollar; some businesses accept euros and yen.

Power

120 volts.

Holidays

Attractions and restaurants may close on national holidays.

Safety

Violent crime is very rare, but pickpocketing and petty crime have increased.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak English; less English is spoken in rural areas.

Healthcare

One hospital and several clinics are available.
